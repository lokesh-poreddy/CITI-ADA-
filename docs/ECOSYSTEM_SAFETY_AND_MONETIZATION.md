# Ecosystem safety, trust and sustainability rules

## Learners and facilitators
- Keep student records minimal. Prefer alias or platform learner IDs over legal names in cohort demos.
- Record consent state before linking a learner or making a recommendation referral.
- Restrict facilitator views to their assigned cohort after a real identity/authorization layer is introduced.
- Do not store government identity numbers, credentials, financial documents or detailed sensitive records in this prototype.

## Mentors
- Mentor profiles are not publicly discoverable until verified.
- The UI displays seeded example mentor cards only as illustrative demo profiles.
- Initial contact is routed through facilitator review; no personal contact fields are exposed.
- Before a field pilot, add mentor identity/organization verification, safeguarding policy, report/block workflow, audit trail, consent/guardian handling and an incident response route.

## Employers and opportunities
- Partner listing drafts are never public before approval.
- HTTPS is a minimum URL check; it is not proof that a company or vacancy is legitimate.
- Review the organization, official source, application flow, eligibility, location, deadline and any stipend/pay statements.
- Distinguish illustrative seed listings from current real vacancies at every display point.

## External integrations and AI
- Listing an adapter is not proof of official partnership, API availability or permission to reuse data.
- Credentials stay server-side and should be injected from a secret manager/environment, not committed.
- Preserve source URL, owner, checked date, expiry, allowed usage and review status for imported records.
- Do not allow AI to invent vacancy status, eligibility rules, deadlines or government benefits.

## Notifications
- This phase supports in-app reminders only. Email/SMS flags do not send messages.
- Obtain meaningful consent before adding any external delivery provider; include frequency controls, unsubscribe, delivery logs and data minimization.

## Sustainability
- Candidate models: CSR/foundation sponsorship, institutional/NGO licenses, facilitator-created modules and transparent partner services.
- Treat each as a hypothesis until interviews, pilot evidence and unit economics support it.
- Keep essential student discovery and access to public opportunities free in the proposed baseline.
- Paid placement must not affect career recommendation ranking, eligibility decisions or public-opportunity visibility.
- No checkout, payment processing, financial account storage or revenue realization is implemented in this increment.
